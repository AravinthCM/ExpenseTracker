# Privacy Policy for ExpenseTracker

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

ExpenseTracker ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ExpenseTracker Android application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

### Information Collection and Use
While using our application, we collect your Google account email and profile information solely for authentication purposes via Google Sign-In. 

### Data Storage and Cloud Sync
Your financial and expense data is stored locally on your device and may be synchronized or backed up to your personal cloud storage (such as Google Drive) to ensure your data is preserved. We do not store your financial data on our own servers, and we cannot access, view, or sell any files stored in your personal Google Drive account.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

### Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
